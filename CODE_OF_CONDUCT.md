# Code of conduct

Veyra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub (**Security → Report a vulnerability** can also be used for
conduct reports that need privacy). Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may ban participants who break this code.
